Shakespeare Explorers

Two web tools for exploring all 37 of Shakespeare's plays, built through AI-assisted vibe coding.

Shakespeare Sentiment Explorer

Draw and compare the emotional arcs of the plays with VADER and a deep-learning model, by sentence, speech, or scene.

Open the tool →

Shakespeare Character Networks Explorer

Follow stage and speech networks of characters across scenes and acts, with centrality, communities, and character focus.
